# Graphic Design

## Project

**Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience**

## Design Goal

The visual design supports a modern, welcoming, and technology-focused learning experience for undergraduate iOS Bootcamp participants. The course uses clear hierarchy, consistent interaction patterns, and structured content to make product-development concepts easy to understand and apply.

## Visual Style

The course uses a clean and professional digital-learning style that reflects innovation, collaboration, and app development.

Key visual characteristics include:

- Clear lesson titles and section headings
- Consistent layouts across lessons
- Structured content blocks to reduce cognitive load
- Interactive components that encourage learner participation
- Visual cues for progress, navigation, and feedback
- Images and illustrations that reinforce teamwork, technology, and innovation

## Typography

Typography is used to create a clear hierarchy and improve readability.

- **Headings:** Large, bold, and easy to scan
- **Subheadings:** Used to divide content into manageable sections
- **Body text:** Short paragraphs and bullet points for easier reading
- **Button labels:** Clear action-oriented language, such as “Start,” “Continue,” and “Submit”

## Colour and Contrast

The course uses a consistent colour palette to create a cohesive visual identity and distinguish interactive elements from informational content.

Colour is used to:

- Highlight important content
- Make buttons and clickable areas recognisable
- Provide visual feedback during knowledge checks
- Support clear separation between sections
- Maintain contrast for readability

## Interaction Design

The visual design supports the following interactive elements:

### Expandable Sections

Expandable sections allow learners to reveal detailed information when they are ready. This keeps screens uncluttered and gives learners control over the pace of learning.

### Flashcards

Flashcards present short, memorable pieces of information, such as bootcamp norms, interview tips, and competitor-research best practices.

### Process Carousels

Carousels present multi-step workflows one step at a time. They are used for processes such as team formation, selection, and competitor analysis.

### Knowledge Checks

Knowledge checks use clear question layouts, answer options, submit buttons, and immediate feedback to help learners assess their understanding.

## Accessibility Considerations

The course design considers accessibility and usability by including:

- Clear and descriptive headings
- Consistent navigation patterns
- Readable text with adequate spacing
- Short, chunked content instead of long paragraphs
- Clearly labelled buttons and interactions
- Written explanations to support visual content
- Feedback that explains learner choices
- Logical lesson progression and visible progress indicators

## Design Principles Applied

- **Consistency:** Similar layouts, buttons, and interaction styles are used throughout the course.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Important information is prioritised through headings, spacing, and layout.
- **Chunking:** Complex concepts are divided into smaller, manageable sections.
- **Learner control:** Learners can explore expandable content and interactive elements at their own pace.
- **Meaningful feedback:** Knowledge checks reinforce correct understanding and address misconceptions.
