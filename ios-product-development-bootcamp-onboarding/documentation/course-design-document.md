# Course Design Document

## Course Title

**Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience**

## Project Overview

This self-paced e-learning course prepares undergraduate students for the iOS Product Development Bootcamp, an intensive three-day learning experience focused on collaborative app innovation.

The course introduces learners to the structured product-development journey, from team formation and brainstorming to user research, competitor analysis, app differentiation, and pitch preparation. It provides foundational knowledge, practical tools, and interactive learning activities that help students enter the bootcamp prepared to contribute effectively to their team project.

## Target Audience

The primary audience is undergraduate students selected for the iOS Development Centre Bootcamp.

Learners may have different levels of experience with product development, app design, macOS, Keynote, user research, and collaborative ideation. The course is designed for beginners and provides clear, step-by-step guidance before the live bootcamp begins.

## Learning Need

Bootcamp participants need a consistent onboarding experience that explains programme expectations and introduces the key product-thinking skills required for their team project.

Without this preparation, students may begin the bootcamp without a shared understanding of teamwork expectations, effective brainstorming, user-centred design, market research, or pitch preparation. This course creates that common foundation.

## Course Goal

By the end of the course, learners will be prepared to participate confidently in the iOS Product Development Bootcamp and contribute to the development and presentation of a user-centred app concept.

## Delivery Format

- **Format:** Self-paced e-learning
- **Authoring tool:** Articulate Storyline 360
- **Learners:** Undergraduate iOS Bootcamp participants
- **Course structure:** Seven lessons and a final quiz
- **Completion requirement:** Pass the final quiz with a score of 80% or higher
- **Deployment:** SCORM package for LMS delivery

## Instructional Strategy

The course uses an interactive, learner-centred approach that includes:

- Short instructional sections to introduce key concepts
- Expandable content for optional detail
- Flashcards to reinforce norms, tips, and best practices
- Process carousels to explain multi-step workflows
- Scenario-based knowledge checks to promote application
- Immediate feedback to correct misconceptions
- A final quiz to assess overall understanding

## Course Topics

1. Bootcamp overview and team formation  
2. Brainstorming norms, macOS, and Keynote basics  
3. Identifying challenges and framing problem statements  
4. User research and persona development  
5. Market research and app differentiators  
6. Pitch preparation and Keynote presentation  
7. Final quiz  

## Success Criteria

The course will be successful when learners can:

- Explain the bootcamp structure and expectations.
- Collaborate effectively with a team partner.
- Apply brainstorming norms during ideation.
- Use basic product-development research methods.
- Identify market gaps and define an app differentiator.
- Prepare a clear and persuasive app pitch.
- Achieve at least 80% on the final quiz.
