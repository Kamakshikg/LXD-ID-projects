# Articulate Storyline Development

## Project

**Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience**

## Development Tool

**Articulate Storyline 360**

## Development Overview

This course was developed as an interactive, self-paced e-learning experience for undergraduate students preparing for the iOS Product Development Bootcamp.

The development focused on transforming instructional content into an engaging digital learning journey. Each lesson was designed to support the learner’s progression from understanding bootcamp expectations to applying product-development methods and preparing an app pitch.

## Course Structure

The course contains seven lessons:

1. Bootcamp Overview and Team Formation  
2. Brainstorming Norms and Programming Tools: macOS & Keynote Basics  
3. Identifying Real-World Challenges and Framing Problem Statements  
4. User Research and Persona Development with the App Design Workbook  
5. Market Research and Defining Differentiators  
6. Pitch Preparation: Designing and Delivering Your App Keynote  
7. Final Quiz  

## Storyline Features Used

### Slide Layouts and Navigation

Consistent slide layouts and navigation controls were used to create a predictable learner experience. Learners can progress through the course in a logical sequence while monitoring their completion progress.

### Interactive States and Triggers

Interactive elements use Storyline states and triggers to reveal information, provide feedback, and guide navigation.

Examples include:

- Buttons that change states when selected
- Expandable sections that reveal additional content
- Flashcards that flip to show explanations
- Navigation triggers for moving between slides and lessons
- Submit triggers for knowledge-check questions

### Flashcard Interactions

Flashcards were used to present concise, memorable information.

Examples include:

- Bootcamp norms and expectations
- Brainstorming and ideation guidance
- User-interview best practices
- Competitor-research tips and common pitfalls

### Process Carousels

Carousels were used to simplify complex processes by presenting one step at a time.

Examples include:

- Bootcamp selection process
- Team-formation process
- Competitor-analysis process

### Knowledge Checks

Multiple-choice knowledge checks were added throughout the course to help learners apply important concepts.

Each knowledge check includes:

- A realistic question or scenario
- Multiple answer options
- A submit button
- Immediate feedback
- A retry option where appropriate

### Final Assessment

A final quiz was developed to assess learning across the full course.

- **Question type:** Multiple choice
- **Topics covered:** Teamwork, brainstorming, macOS basics, user research, personas, market research, differentiators, and pitching
- **Passing score:** 80%
- **Results:** Learners receive a score and can retry if needed

## Quality Assurance

The course was reviewed and tested to confirm:

- All buttons and navigation controls function correctly
- Interactive elements display the intended states and feedback
- Knowledge-check answers score correctly
- The final quiz accurately calculates results
- Slide layouts are visually consistent
- Text is clear and readable
- Course flow is logical across all seven lessons
- Completion requirements are aligned with the final assessment
