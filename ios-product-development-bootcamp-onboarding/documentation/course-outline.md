# Course Outline

## Course Title

**Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience**

## Course Overview

This seven-lesson onboarding course prepares undergraduate students for the iOS Product Development Bootcamp. It introduces learners to collaboration, brainstorming, problem framing, user research, market research, app differentiation, and pitch preparation.

---

## Lesson 1: Bootcamp Overview and Team Formation

### Purpose
Introduce learners to the bootcamp experience, its expectations, and the importance of teamwork.

### Topics
- Welcome to the iOS Product Development Bootcamp
- The value of collaborative innovation
- Bootcamp structure and product-development journey
- Selection process and orientation
- Bootcamp norms and expectations
- Team-formation process
- Effective collaboration practices

### Learning Activities
- Expandable content sections
- Process carousel for the selection process
- Flashcards for bootcamp norms
- Process carousel for team formation

---

## Lesson 2: Brainstorming Norms and Programming Tools: macOS & Keynote Basics

### Purpose
Help learners brainstorm effectively and build familiarity with the tools used during the bootcamp.

### Topics
- The role of brainstorming in product development
- Eight brainstorming norms
- Domains for ideation
- macOS Desktop, Dock, and Menu Bar
- Essential shortcuts and gestures
- Keynote basics for presentation design

### Learning Activities
- Expandable brainstorming-norm sections
- Ideation-domain flashcards
- Interactive tool-guidance content
- Knowledge check

---

## Lesson 3: Identifying Real-World Challenges and Framing Problem Statements

### Purpose
Help learners move from broad observations to focused, user-centred problem statements.

### Topics
- Identifying meaningful real-world challenges
- Understanding users and contexts
- Distinguishing problems from solutions
- Narrowing a broad topic
- Writing a clear problem statement
- Connecting a problem statement to an app opportunity

### Learning Activities
- Guided examples
- Step-by-step problem-framing content
- Knowledge check

---

## Lesson 4: User Research and Persona Development with the App Design Workbook

### Purpose
Teach learners how to gather user insights and translate research into actionable personas.

### Topics
- Importance of empathy in app development
- User interviews
- Observational research
- Surveys
- Desk research
- Open-ended interview questions
- Avoiding leading questions
- Capturing real user stories
- Creating user personas

### Learning Activities
- Expandable research-method sections
- Interview best-practice flashcards
- Scenario-based multiple-choice question
- Persona-development guidance

---

## Lesson 5: Market Research and Defining Differentiators

### Purpose
Help learners understand the competitive landscape and define what makes their app idea valuable.

### Topics
- Purpose of market research
- Identifying relevant competitor apps
- Reviewing competitor features and functionality
- Evaluating strengths and weaknesses
- Analysing user experience and reviews
- Identifying market gaps
- Defining a value proposition
- Creating app differentiators

### Learning Activities
- Competitor-analysis process carousel
- Expandable competitor-review criteria
- Research tips and pitfalls flashcards
- Scenario-based knowledge check

---

## Lesson 6: Pitch Preparation: Designing and Delivering Your App Keynote

### Purpose
Prepare learners to present their app idea clearly and persuasively.

### Topics
- Purpose of an app pitch
- Structuring a winning pitch
- Product storytelling
- Designing impactful Keynote slides
- Preparing for presentation delivery
- Receiving and using feedback

### Learning Activities
- Guided pitch-preparation content
- Presentation-design tips
- Reflection and preparation prompts

---

## Lesson 7: Final Quiz

### Purpose
Assess learner understanding of the bootcamp process and core product-development concepts.

### Topics Assessed
- Teamwork and bootcamp expectations
- Team formation
- Brainstorming norms
- macOS and Keynote basics
- User research and personas
- Market and competitor analysis
- Value propositions and differentiators
- Pitch preparation

### Assessment Details
- Question type: Multiple choice
- Passing score: 80%
- Feedback: Provided after quiz completion
- Completion requirement: Achieve 80% or higher
