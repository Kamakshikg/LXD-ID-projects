# Storyboard

## Assessment Strategy

The course includes formative knowledge checks throughout the learning journey. Learners receive immediate feedback after answering questions, helping them correct misconceptions before progressing.

The final quiz serves as the summative assessment. Learners must achieve a score of at least **80%** to pass the course.

## Interaction Strategy

The course uses several interaction types to maintain learner engagement:

- **Expandable sections:** Allow learners to reveal supporting information at their own pace.
- **Flashcards:** Present concise tips, norms, and best practices in a memorable format.
- **Process carousels:** Break complex multi-step processes into manageable stages.
- **Knowledge checks:** Encourage learners to apply concepts through decisions and scenarios.
- **Final quiz:** Verifies learners’ readiness for the bootcamp.
