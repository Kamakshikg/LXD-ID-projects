# Learning Objectives

## Course-Level Learning Objectives

By the end of the **Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience** course, learners will be able to:

1. Describe the structure, purpose, and expectations of the iOS Product Development Bootcamp.

2. Explain the importance of collaboration and apply effective team-working practices during bootcamp activities.

3. Apply eight brainstorming norms to generate ideas respectfully, creatively, and productively.

4. Navigate essential macOS features and use Keynote basics to support project work and presentations.

5. Identify real-world challenges and write focused, user-centred problem statements.

6. Conduct basic user research using interviews, observation, surveys, and desk research.

7. Create a user persona using research findings and the App Design Workbook.

8. Analyse competitor apps to identify strengths, weaknesses, user needs, and market gaps.

9. Define a clear value proposition and differentiators for an app idea.

10. Structure and deliver a concise, persuasive pitch for an app concept.

11. Demonstrate understanding of the course content by achieving a score of at least 80% on the final quiz.

## Lesson-Level Objectives

### Lesson 1: Bootcamp Overview and Team Formation

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Explain the bootcamp structure and expected outcomes.
- Identify key bootcamp norms, including attendance, punctuality, participation, and team engagement.
- Describe the team-formation process.
- Recognise the value of communication, mutual respect, and shared responsibility.

### Lesson 2: Brainstorming Norms and Programming Tools

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Apply brainstorming norms during collaborative ideation.
- Identify areas of opportunity for app innovation.
- Use basic macOS navigation and productivity features.
- Use Keynote tools and shortcuts to support presentation development.

### Lesson 3: Identifying Challenges and Framing Problem Statements

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Identify meaningful real-world challenges.
- Distinguish between broad topics and focused user problems.
- Write a clear and actionable problem statement.

### Lesson 4: User Research and Persona Development

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Identify appropriate user-research methods.
- Use open-ended questions during user interviews.
- Avoid leading questions and capture authentic user insights.
- Create a user persona based on research findings.

### Lesson 5: Market Research and Defining Differentiators

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Identify relevant competitor apps.
- Evaluate competitors’ features, user experience, reviews, and accessibility.
- Identify gaps and opportunities in the market.
- Define a value proposition and meaningful app differentiators.

### Lesson 6: Pitch Preparation

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Organise an app pitch into a clear and logical structure.
- Use storytelling to communicate an app idea.
- Design effective presentation slides in Keynote.
- Prepare to present confidently and respond to feedback.
