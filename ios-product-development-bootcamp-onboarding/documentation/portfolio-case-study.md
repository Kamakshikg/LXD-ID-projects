# Portfolio Case Study

## Kickstart to iOS Product Development Journey: Bootcamp Onboarding Experience

## Project Summary

This project is an interactive e-learning onboarding course created to prepare undergraduate students for the iOS Product Development Bootcamp. The course guides learners through the early stages of product development, from team formation and brainstorming to user research, competitor analysis, app differentiation, and pitch preparation.

[Launch the live course](https://6a644c2213e47707e2227e4f--iridescent-narwhal-e461d6.netlify.app)

## The Challenge

Students selected for the iOS Product Development Bootcamp come from different backgrounds and levels of experience. Before beginning an intensive, three-day collaborative bootcamp, they need a shared understanding of the programme structure, teamwork expectations, product-thinking methods, and required tools.

The challenge was to create an engaging onboarding experience that would prepare learners to participate confidently and contribute effectively to a team-based app-development project.

## The Solution

I designed and developed a seven-lesson self-paced e-learning course that introduces learners to the complete early-stage app-product journey.

The course provides clear guidance on:

- Bootcamp expectations and team formation
- Collaborative brainstorming practices
- macOS and Keynote basics
- Identifying real-world challenges
- Writing user-centred problem statements
- Conducting user research
- Creating user personas
- Analysing competitor apps
- Defining app differentiators
- Preparing and delivering an app pitch

## Instructional Design Approach

The course follows a learner-centred approach that connects each lesson to the real tasks students will complete during the bootcamp.

Learners first understand the bootcamp context and collaboration expectations. They then build essential skills in brainstorming, problem framing, user research, market research, and pitching. This sequence mirrors a realistic product-development workflow and helps learners see how each stage builds on the previous one.

## Key Interactions

To support active learning and avoid long blocks of text, the course includes:

- Expandable sections for exploring detailed information
- Flashcards for bootcamp norms, interview guidance, and research tips
- Process carousels for multi-step workflows
- Scenario-based multiple-choice knowledge checks
- Immediate feedback for learner decisions
- A final quiz with an 80% passing score

## Example Learning Interaction

In the market-research lesson, learners compare competitor apps and identify the most useful insight for shaping their own app direction. The activity reinforces that learners should not simply copy competitors; they should identify gaps, improve the user experience, and create a meaningful differentiator.
